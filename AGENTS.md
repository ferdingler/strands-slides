# Slide guidance

When creating or revising a deck:

- Use one idea per slide.
- Use one dominant visual or concrete example.
- Aim for 20–35 visible words, including the title and diagram labels. Title slides
  may use fewer words. Count slide content separately from recurring navigation
  and footer text.
- Put explanations and qualifications in speaker notes.
- Keep the Strands fonts and colors: JetBrains Mono for headings and code,
  Space Grotesk for body text, black backgrounds, `#00cc60` headings, and white
  or `#a78bfa` supporting text.

Before finishing, count the rendered words and inspect every slide. Check for
readable labels, generous whitespace, and a clear focal point as well as overflow.
Build the selected deck and verify its preview and PDF export.
